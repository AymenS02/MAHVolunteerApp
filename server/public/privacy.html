Privacy Policy

MAH Volunteer app · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains what information the MAH Volunteer app collects, how it is used, who can see it, and how you can delete it. The app is operated by [ORGANIZATION LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") to organize volunteers for our events.

Who can use the app

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

Information you give us when you sign up or edit your profile:

  • First and last name
  • Email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form)
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Whether you volunteer as a brother or a sister (events have separate spots for each)
  • Whether you are a high school student (optional)

Information created when you use the app:

  • The events you register for, join the waitlist for, or cancel
  • Your volunteer hours, including hours approved by organizers and any adjustments they make, with the reason they give
  • If you turn on notifications: a push notification token for your device and its type (iPhone or Android), and a record of which reminders were sent to you so you don't get duplicates

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run your account and let you sign up for events
  • To let organizers manage events, contact volunteers, and confirm volunteer hours
  • To keep a record of your volunteer hours (for example, for school community-service requirements)
  • To send notifications you have turned on: reminders, hours approvals, changes to events you signed up for, waitlist updates, and messages from organizers
  • To keep the service secure (for example, limiting repeated failed logins)

Who can see your information

  • You can see all of your own information in the app.
  • Event organizers (admins) can see the name, phone number, group, date of birth and age, registration status, and volunteer hours of people signed up for their events, so they can run the event and confirm hours.
  • Other volunteers cannot see your information. They only see how many spots are filled.
  • If you register for an event, you see the organizer contact for your group.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • MongoDB Atlas – stores the app's database
  • [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Render] – runs the app's server
  • Expo, together with Apple and Google – deliver push notifications to your device

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and profile, removes you from all events and waitlists, deletes your devices' notification tokens, and deletes your volunteer-hours records. It cannot be undone. Copies may remain in encrypted database backups for a limited time until those backups expire.

You can also ask us to delete your account by contacting us at the address below.

Your choices

  • Edit your name and phone number in Profile → Edit profile. To correct your date of birth, contact an organizer.
  • Turn all notifications on or off in Profile → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Access to volunteer information is limited to event organizers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
