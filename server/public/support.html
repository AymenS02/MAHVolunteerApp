Common questions

  • I forgot my password. Email us from the address on your account and we'll help you get back in.
  • My hours look wrong. Your Profile shows every event and adjustment behind your total. Contact the event organizer if something is missing.
  • My date of birth is wrong. Ask an organizer to correct it.
  • I'm not getting notifications. Check Profile → Notifications in the app and your phone's notification settings.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Delete account. See our privacy policy for what this removes.
